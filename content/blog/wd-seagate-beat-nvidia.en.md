When ChatGPT debuted in November 2022 and set every investor’s imagination on fire, the winners looked obvious: Nvidia, supplying the GPUs behind every large language model, and Micron, feeding them memory. Capital flooded in. Everyone knew the story.

What fewer people noticed is what happened to Western Digital and Seagate — two names most people last think about when they buy a hard drive. According to Yahoo Finance data, from ChatGPT’s launch through today, these two “old” storage companies have outpaced both Nvidia **and** Micron on total returns. And not by a hair.

## When AI turns a hard drive into strategic infrastructure

This is less glamorous than headline AI — but arguably more consequential. Artificial intelligence rewrote demand across the hardware stack, not just at the top. AI servers gulp enormous quantities of memory and storage, far beyond what the industry modeled. Demand exploded before supply caught up. Suddenly disks became bottleneck products — not unlike Nvidia GPUs.

Much of the real rally clustered around April 2025. That month also marked the rebound in memory equities after lows around the tariff shock. Stretch the timeline back to November 2022, though, and the storage majors still stand out.

## $440 billion in a single trading day

The story is bigger than HDDs alone. Markets are repricing **quiet hardware**: memory, networking, optics, foundational silicon. Micron vaulted 15.52% one Friday alone; from its March 2025 low it climbed roughly **130%**, adding on the order of **$470 billion** in market value. Intel is up nearly **200%** from that low; AMD joined Sandisk in triple?digit climbs. Lumentum, making optical components for AI infrastructure, is riding a thirteen?month streak of weekly gains.

Nvidia hasn’t vanished, of course. From the March 2025 trough alone it added more than **$1 trillion** in market capitalization — scale with few historical parallels. Yet anyone hunting “smart money” rotations is staring past pure graphics plays into suppliers that barely showed up in AI conversations a year ago.

The market absorbed a slower lesson a tech cycle needs more than marquee chips — it needs roads, warehousing, routing, and reservoirs. Whoever owns the plumbing owns part of the next decade’s bargaining power.
